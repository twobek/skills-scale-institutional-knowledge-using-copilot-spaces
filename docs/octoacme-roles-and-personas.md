# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Works with Quality Assurance Lead** on acceptance criteria and test coverage standards
- **Works with Technical Architect/Engineering Lead** on design reviews and technical guidance
- **Works with Product Managers** on feature requirements and acceptance criteria
- **Works with Project Managers** on task estimates and scheduling
- **Works with Scrum Master** on sprint planning and impediment resolution

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Works with Stakeholder/Product Owner Representative** to gather business requirements and manage expectations
- **Works with Quality Assurance Lead** to define acceptance criteria and quality metrics
- **Works with Technical Architect/Engineering Lead** on feasibility assessments and technical trade-offs
- **Works with Developers** on feature scope and requirements
- **Works with Project Managers** on planning and delivery timelines

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Works with Scrum Master** on sprint execution and process improvements
- **Works with Stakeholder/Product Owner Representative** on scope management and stakeholder alignment
- **Works with Technical Architect/Engineering Lead** on technical dependencies and risk mitigation
- **Works with Quality Assurance Lead** on quality metrics and release readiness
- **Works with all team members** on scheduling and status tracking

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads define quality standards, establish testing strategies, and validate deliverables against acceptance criteria. They ensure product reliability and user satisfaction through comprehensive quality frameworks.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards, testing strategies, and acceptance criteria
- Coordinate testing activities and manage QA resources
- Identify quality risks and track defects
- Validate releases and conduct post-release monitoring
- Collaborate with developers and product managers on quality trade-offs

### Goals
- Ensure product meets quality and reliability standards
- Reduce production defects and support costs
- Improve user confidence and satisfaction

### Typical Communication
- Quality metric reviews and risk escalations
- Test plans and defect reports
- Release readiness assessments

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Works with Developers** on test coverage, acceptance criteria validation, and defect resolution
- **Works with Product Managers** on quality metrics and acceptance criteria definition
- **Works with Project Managers** on release planning and quality gates
- **Works with Technical Architect/Engineering Lead** on test strategy and performance standards
- **Works with Stakeholder/Product Owner Representative** on quality expectations and release validation

---

## Technical Architect/Engineering Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Architects provide strategic technical direction, guide architectural decisions, and ensure system scalability and maintainability. They bridge business requirements with technical feasibility.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical strategy and system architecture
- Lead technical design reviews and technology evaluations
- Identify technical risks and propose solutions
- Mentor developers and promote best practices
- Ensure code quality and system performance standards

### Goals
- Build scalable, maintainable, and performant systems
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Enable faster delivery through architectural guidance

### Typical Communication
- Architecture decision records (ADRs) and design reviews
- Technical risk assessments and mitigation plans
- Technology evaluation and tool recommendations

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Works with Developers** on design reviews, technical mentoring, and code quality standards
- **Works with Product Managers** on technical feasibility assessments and trade-off analysis
- **Works with Project Managers** on technical risk identification and mitigation planning
- **Works with Quality Assurance Lead** on performance standards and test strategy
- **Works with Stakeholder/Product Owner Representative** on technical constraints and scalability concerns

---

## Scrum Master/Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile processes, remove team impediments, and drive continuous improvement. They enable teams to self-organize and deliver value consistently within sprint cycles.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, and retrospectives
- Remove blockers and impediments to team progress
- Coach team members on agile practices and frameworks
- Track sprint velocity and identify process improvements
- Foster collaboration and psychological safety within the team

### Goals
- Enable consistent sprint execution and predictable delivery
- Improve team velocity and efficiency over time
- Build a high-performing, self-organizing team

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Retrospective summaries and improvement actions
- Impediment escalations and process metrics

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Works with Project Managers** on sprint planning, scheduling, and dependency management
- **Works with all team members** on removing impediments and facilitating collaboration
- **Works with Developers** on sprint execution and technical impediment resolution
- **Works with Product Managers** on backlog refinement and sprint goal alignment
- **Works with Stakeholder/Product Owner Representative** on sprint reviews and feedback incorporation

---

## Stakeholder/Product Owner Representative

### Role Summary
Stakeholder/Product Owner Representatives act as the voice of business stakeholders, translating business needs into requirements and managing expectations across delivery and business teams. They ensure alignment between business goals and delivery outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and communicate business requirements and priorities
- Represent stakeholder interests in project decisions
- Manage scope changes and trade-offs between stakeholders and delivery team
- Validate deliverables against business expectations
- Facilitate communication between business and delivery teams

### Goals
- Ensure business requirements are clearly understood and met
- Minimize scope creep and misaligned deliverables
- Maintain stakeholder satisfaction and confidence

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder requirement sessions and feedback reviews
- Scope change requests and impact assessments
- Release validation and sign-off communications

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Works with Product Managers** to refine requirements and prioritize features
- **Works with Project Managers** on scope management and dependency communication
- **Works with Developers** to clarify business requirements and acceptance criteria
- **Works with Quality Assurance Lead** on business validation and acceptance sign-off
- **Works with Scrum Master** on sprint reviews and feedback incorporation
